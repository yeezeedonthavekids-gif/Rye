Looks

Pick a look. The whole browser follows.

Tabs, toolbar, menus, dialogs and settings all take the same colors. Choose light, dark or match your system, any accent, and how round you like your corners.

Rye — warm neutrals and a quiet blue.
What you get

Small things, done with care.

A greeting that changes

A different line each time you open a tab, set in a proper serif, with your name in it.

Shortcuts with real icons

Each site's own icon, or your own image and color. Drag to reorder and group them into collections.

A floating page

The page sits as a rounded sheet inside the window, the way Rye has always looked.

Backgrounds

Flat artwork and textures, a solid color, or a photo of your own. No stock wallpapers.

Profiles

Separate names, looks and new tab pages for work and home, and switch in a click.

Undo for everything

Every change in Personalize can be undone or reset. Export your look and bring it to another computer.

Personalize

One page for all of it.

Rye asks five quick questions the first time you open it. Everything after that lives in Personalize, with a live preview beside it.

  • Name, greeting style and text color
  • Seven looks, any accent, or monochrome
  • Soft, Rounded or Extra round corners
  • Clock, date, a note for today, bookmarks and history

Privacy

Your name stays on your computer.

  • Rye has no telemetry of its own.
  • Your name, shortcuts, photo and settings are stored on your device and nowhere else.
  • Site icons come from the sites you add, and only once.
  • Background model downloads and page preloading are off.
